#### Printables vices design challenge
this month's designer challenge on printables are Vises **(and other work holding solution)** so i tried my best to come up with something creative, but first i checked out the competition and two models caught my eye:
- [normally closed clamp by NomeUn](https://www.printables.com/model/1900742-normally-closed-clamp) here is my [make](https://www.printables.com/make/3703524) 
- [pocket size third hand mini spring loaded vise by Rjun](https://www.printables.com/model/1901402-pocket-size-third-hand-mini-spring-loaded-vise) here is my [make](https://www.printables.com/make/3703408)

i'm not going to deep in why those caught my eye, you can check my make for that, but mainly because i saw the vision of the designers, and i felt like it really fitted me:
- easy to print, all printed
- useful and versatile
- quick to print
- clean design, no overcomplication

so with this objectives in mind i came up with two models, i know, shocker.
##### [PCB thread Vise](https://www.printables.com/model/1901721-pcb-thread-vise)
this was my first idea, it came to me because i needed something to hold a motherboard that my girlfriend (hiii) was throwing away and i thought i could fix it (more on that in the upcoming week hopefully) and so keeping in mind my keypoints i opened fusion and i came up with this:![[Pasted image 20261113123901.png]]
![[Pasted image 20261113123914.png]]

and it looked pretty good, if i can say so myself. Basically is just a empty cylinder with a thread on knurled cap and it works by pinching the board between the cap and the body, you can put stuff in it to make it heavier and when not in use it can double as storage so it doesn't take up useful space on your desk, i found that putting the copper sponges to clean the tip of soldering irons is also quite a good idea which was suggested to me by my roommate.
This model has also gained quite a traction with 50 likes and 60 downloads which for me is crazy, it is my best model so far
##### [fully 3d printed butt clamp](https://www.printables.com/model/1904001-fully-3d-printed-butt-clamp)
this is also a design that came out of necessity, some of you may be aware that i built myself a custom keyboard and i needed a way to join the 2 case parts together so i came up with this![[Pasted image 20261113124443.png]]
if some of you are welders and have worked with sheet metal you will recognize this design immediately, basically how it works is that you can uncouple the straight bar at the end from the thin plate connected to the screw, than you can slide said plate between 2 plates that are butting against each other (hence the name), you then slide the bar through again and tighten it holding the two plate parallel and with a small gap between each other, you can than weld them together, or glue them, or whatever, i technically soldered them together since they were 2 pla parts and so i used my pinecil and some filament to fill the gap and join them together, and it worked great, here is a picture
